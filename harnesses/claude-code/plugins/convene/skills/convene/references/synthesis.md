# Synthesis

The synthesis is yours, not the engine's. Write `synthesis.md` in the
export directory.

**Structure.** Findings first, most severe first, each with: the claim
in one line; which seats raised it (`skeptic`, `maintainer`), or that
you raised it yourself; the evidence; whether you verified it against
the repository; and what would settle it. Then disagreements, with your
ruling or your reason for not ruling. Then coverage: what the panel
checked and what it did not. Then the receipts, copied from
`convene status`, and every red flag verbatim.

**Attribute honestly.** A finding is a seat's until you verified it.
Say "the skeptic (Opus) found and I confirmed" or "the maintainer
(gpt-5.5) claims; I could not reproduce". Never present a seat's claim
as your own observation.

**Do not launder.** If a seat compacted, ran unisolated, or served a
model by alias, the synthesis says so where the seat's findings appear.
A synthesis that reads clean because it dropped the receipts is worse
than no synthesis.

**Keep your own findings separate.** You read the board with the
repository open; you will see things the seats missed. Report them,
labeled as yours, so the reader can tell the panel's coverage from
your own.

**Report to the user in this order:** the verdict in one paragraph;
the findings list; the receipts and red flags; where the export is.
